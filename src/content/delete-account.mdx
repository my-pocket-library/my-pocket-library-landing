## Delete your account in the app

- **iPhone:** tap your avatar, then **Account**, then **Delete account**.
- **Android:** tap your avatar, then **Delete account**.

You will be asked to confirm with your password or by signing in again. Deletion starts at once and signs you out when it finishes.

## Ask us to delete it

If you can't open the app, email **[hasanharman33@gmail.com](mailto:hasanharman33@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20account)** from the email address your account uses, with the subject "Delete my account". If you signed in with Apple using a hidden email address, tell us your username instead. We may ask you to confirm, then delete the account within one month.

## What is deleted

Your profile, username, library, notes, shelves and locations, wishlist, reading activity and activity likes, friend records and requests, book-borrow requests, shared book loans, book club memberships, support requests and catalog feedback, push tokens, and every conversation you are part of. If someone has blocked you, only the messages you sent in that conversation are removed. Your sign-in is deleted, My Pocket Library's access to your Google account is revoked if you signed in with Google, and your subscription record at RevenueCat is deleted.

## What is kept, and for how long

- **Abuse reports** you filed, or that were filed about you, are kept for up to two years so we can recognise repeat behaviour.
- **Fingerprints of removed photos** (a hash, not the photo) are kept for up to two years.
- **Backups:** deleted data stays in our database's one-hour version history, then it is gone for good.
- **Purchases:** Apple and Google keep their own records of what you bought. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.
