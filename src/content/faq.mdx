<FaqItem question="How does the scanning work?">
  Point your phone camera at a book — the spine, the front cover, or the
  barcode on the back. Our on-device model reads the title, author, and
  ISBN in about a second and matches it against a library of over five
  million books. Tap once to confirm and it's in your shelf.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What if a book doesn't get recognised?">
  You can still add it manually — title, author, and an optional cover
  photo are enough. Anything you add by hand is tagged so we can improve
  the model on subsequent scans of similar editions.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is My Pocket Library free?">
  Yes. The free tier covers up to 200 scanned books, reading-status
  tracking, and full-text search. Pocket Library Pro ($3.99/mo) lifts
  that ceiling, syncs your shelves across devices, and unlocks
  reading-pace stats and Goodreads/Hardcover import.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Where is my library data stored?">
  Locally on your phone by default — Pocket Library works offline, and
  your shelf never leaves the device unless you turn sync on. Pro sync
  uses end-to-end encryption: we can't read your library, only your
  authenticated devices can.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I export my library?">
  Anytime. Settings → Library → Export gives you a CSV or a JSON dump
  with everything: titles, authors, ISBNs, statuses, dates, ratings,
  and notes. You own your data.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Do you support Android?">
  iOS is live today, Android is in beta with TestFlight-equivalent
  rolling invites — join the waitlist from the Get the App button up
  top and we'll send a build the next round we open.
</FaqItem>
