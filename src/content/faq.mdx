<FaqItem question="How does the scanning work?">
  Use your camera to scan ISBN barcodes or book spines. Scanning happens
  on your device; scan images are never uploaded or stored. Book metadata
  is looked up through Google Books and Open Library.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What if a book doesn’t get recognized?">
  You can add a book manually and review or edit its details. Metadata from
  third-party catalogs can sometimes be incomplete or incorrect.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What is Pocket Library Pro?">
  Pro is an optional subscription with weekly, monthly, and yearly plans.
  See the app for current prices and features. You can manage or cancel
  your subscription in your App Store account settings.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Where is my library data stored?">
  Your library, profile, and messages are stored in Google Cloud Firestore
  in a European multi-region. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest;
  direct messages are not end-to-end encrypted. See our
  [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for the full details.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I export or delete my data?">
  Yes. In Settings, choose “Export my data” for a JSON copy of your data.
  Account deletion is also available in Settings. Our
  [Privacy Policy](/privacy) explains what is included and how deletion works.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Which platform is the app for?">
  My Pocket Library is an iOS app.
</FaqItem>
