# Policy sources

`privacy-policy.mdx` and `terms-and-license.mdx` reproduce the text from the
Pocket Library app repository's `legal/privacy-policy.md` and
`legal/terms-and-license.md`. Only the title and effective date are rendered
separately by the route's LegalPage wrapper. Keep their text and effective
dates in sync when the app policies change.

Imported September 19, 2026. FAQ and support copy is based on those documents.
